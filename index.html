CMS Advanced

Court of Master Sommeliers — Advanced Sommelier Certificate

The Examination

The Advanced Sommelier Certificate is a 5-day programme (lectures + exams). Pass mark: 60% in each section. All three sections must be passed together.

1. Theory

Multiple choice + short answer questions

2. Tasting

6 wines blind in 25 minutes (oral)

3. Practical Service

45-minute service examination

Legend: Context (Intro + Certified) Advanced Level (study focus) MS Diploma (awareness only)

Deductive Tasting

🎯 Advanced Level (6 wines, 25 minutes, oral)

  • Identify a wide range of grape varietal characteristics
  • Orally describe wines in both technical and marketing format
  • Evaluate sight, nose and palate using Court format
  • Evaluate aromas & flavours: Primary, Secondary, Tertiary elements
  • Evaluate structure (Alcohol, Acidity, Tannins) quantitatively
  • Comment on age characteristics
  • Constructive conclusion: Style, Country/Region, Quality level, Vintage
  • Know and identify wine faults (Brett, Oxidation, Reduction, TCA/Anisole)
  • Identify vinification methods used to correct faults
