Key: Context (Intro + Certified) Advanced Focus

France

01 Alsace

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Factors affecting climate. Importance of the Vosges
  • Sub districts, location of best vineyards & variety of soils
  • Grape varietals
  • Appellations: Alsace, Grand Cru, Crémant, Communale, Lieu dit
  • Styles of wine: VT & SGN. Labelling terms
  • Grand Cru varietals and sites. Yields per hectare
  • Required sugar levels for VT & SGN. Crémant production

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • % plantings of varietals
  • Specific villages with Grand Cru and Clos sites
  • Soil diversity
  • Yields for different quality levels
  • Minimum potential alcohol levels for VT & SGN
  • Vintages
  • Principal producers and sites
  • Latest developments & updates
02 Bordeaux

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Climate, soil types, production & ageing techniques
  • Districts, sub districts. 1855 Classification
  • Top Médoc chateaux. St Émilion classification & "A" Châteaux
  • Top Pomerol Châteaux. Grape varietals & blending
  • Trade structure: En primeur, Négociants
  • 1st–5th classified growths. Cru Bourgeois
  • All sub districts. Commune names. Classic vintages

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • ALL classified growths and their communes (Médoc, Sauternes, St Émilion)
  • St Émilion Classification (full). Satellites. Pomerol Châteaux
  • Sauternes & Pessac-Léognan Châteaux & communes
  • Côtes de Bordeaux classification. Bourg / Blaye wines
  • Characteristics of the wines
  • Vintages 30 years & their characteristics
  • 2nd labels from classified sites
  • Principal producers and sites
03 Burgundy

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Climatic influences. Permitted grapes. Soil types
  • Quality levels: Generic, District, Villages
  • Chablis & quality levels. Côte d'Or communes
  • Premier Cru & Grand Cru wines. Mâcon & Côte Chalonnaise
  • Beaujolais Crus. Smaller districts. Classic vintages

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Grand Cru / 1er Cru wines from principal ACs
  • Pouilly-Fuissé Crus. Beaujolais villages
  • Differentiate Lieu dits vs Premier Cru
  • Noted Clos sites. Noted producers linked to wines/sites
  • Vintages & off vintages of last 30 years
  • Latest developments & updates
04 Champagne

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Climate, sub districts, grapes, soils, production methods
  • Styles, qualities, labelling terms, bottle sizes
  • Yields & extraction. Quality marques. Coteaux Champenois
  • Grand Cru villages. Contribution of each varietal. Dosage. Single vineyards

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • ALL Grand Cru and Premier Cru classifications
  • Special Club producers
  • Characteristics of vintages in past 30 years
  • Quality marques from top producers
  • Principal producers and sites
  • Latest developments & updates
05 Loire

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Climate, soil types, districts & sub districts
  • Grapes & wines. Styles. Principal AOPs
  • Coteaux du Layon, Savennières, Central Vineyards, Anjou-Saumur, Touraine, Muscadet
  • Grape synonyms. Sur lie. Smaller ACs (St Pourçain, Cheverny, Orléans)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Muscadet Crus and ageing requirements
  • Soil types in Sancerre / Pouilly
  • Villages in Layon
  • Soil composition in various districts
  • Notable producers in all districts
  • Coteaux du Layon vintages
  • Latest developments & updates
06 Rhône Valley

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Climate, districts & sub districts. Grapes & blends (South)
  • VDN & VDL production. Principal estates CdP
  • Principal sites North Rhône (Hermitage, Côte-Rôtie)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Mistral. Côtes du Rhône village names
  • Permitted grape blends for principal ACs
  • Smaller ACs (Tricastin, Luberon, etc.)
  • Soil types throughout. Geographical features (Alps, Dents du Montmirail)
  • Climats of the Rhône
  • Principal producers and wines. Vintages
  • Latest developments & updates
07 South of France · Languedoc · Jura · Savoie

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Provence, Languedoc, Roussillon, Bergerac, Madiran, Cahors
  • VDN & VDL wines. Vin de Pays districts
  • Jura: Vin Jaune / Vin de Paille production. Savoie wines

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Languedoc: Major & minor ACs. Languedoc Crus
  • Roussillon, Gaillac, Béarn, Jurançon, Irouléguy
  • Specific villages of Côte de Provence
  • Jura districts. Savoie Crus
  • Styles of VDN / VDL wines
  • Native grape varietals & composition
  • Principal producers all districts. Latest developments
08 Sparkling Wines (Global)

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Methods: Traditional, Charmat, Ancestral. Terms in other countries
  • Crémant, Cava. Quality levels (DOCG, DO, Qualitäts Sekt)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Maturation periods for all quality levels
  • Producers & sites: Prosecco, Franciacorta, Alta Langhe, Tasmania, NZ, USA, SA, UK
  • VDP Sekt regulations
  • Vintages for principal sparkling wines
  • Latest developments & updates

Europe

09 Spain

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Quality structure (DO, DOCa, Vinos de Pagos). Districts & varietals
  • Ageing terms. Principal wines: Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Priorat, Rías Baixas, etc.
  • Cava. DOCas. Sub districts of key regions

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Vinos de Pagos — names, locations
  • Smaller districts in Southern Spain
  • Geographic influences (rivers, mountains, coastal)
  • Cava de Paraje Calificado. Corpinat producers
  • DO zones: Balearic & Canary Islands
  • Txakolina, Bullas, Méntrida, Alella, Empordà, Terra Alta, Conca de Barberà, Costers del Segre, etc.
  • Vintages last 10 years. Latest developments
10 Portugal

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Quality structure (VR & DOC). Grapes. Dão, Minho, Douro, Bairrada, Alentejo
  • Ageing terms (Garrafeira, Reserva). All VR regions and DOCs

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Synonyms for Portuguese varieties
  • Ageing requirements (Seleccionada, Colheita)
  • Sub-regions of DOC districts. Azores wines
  • Douro table wines and producers
  • Vintages. Latest developments
11 Italy

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Quality structure (IGT, DOC, DOCG). Districts & grapes
  • Production: Recioto, Ripasso, Amarone, Vin Santo. Prosecco
  • DOCGs. Ageing (Barolo, Barbaresco, Chianti, Brunello). Sub-districts Chianti

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Sub-districts: Barolo, Barbaresco, Soave, Valpolicella
  • Smaller areas: Liguria, Friuli, Trentino, Bolgheri, Campania, Sicily, Sardinia, etc.
  • Min/max alcohol & ageing for principal DOC/DOCG
  • Grape varieties native to Southern Italy
  • Vintages 20 years DOCG. Super Tuscan & 2nd wines. Passito
  • Principal producers all regions. Latest developments
12 Germany

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Quality structure (Qualitätswein, Prädikatswein). Anbaugebiet
  • Grapes & crossings. Wine villages (Mosel, Rheingau, Pfalz, Rheinhessen)
  • VDP structure: Erste Lage, Grosse Gewächs

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • In depth ALL Anbaugebiet. AP number. Landwein nomenclature
  • VDP grading & approved varietals. Vine crossings & parentage
  • Required sugar levels (Oechsle) for QmP grades
  • Monopole sites. Soils of principal sites
  • Min ageing for Grosses Gewächs. Villages in all Anbaugebiet
  • Vintages 20 years. Latest developments
13 Austria

Context (Intro + Certified)

  • Pannonian effect. Quality: QbA, QmP, Ausbruch, Strohwein
  • DAC structure. Wachau terms. Grapes & districts

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Traditionsweingüter & Erste Lage sites
  • Austrian Sekt quality hierarchy & maturation
  • Principal sites: Wachau, Kamptal, Kremstal. Soils
  • Native varieties & crossings. Vintages 10 years
  • Latest developments
14 Greece · Switzerland · England · Eastern Europe

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Greece: Production areas, unique techniques (Santorini training), producers, soils
  • Switzerland: ConfidenCiel, Ticino, Bündner Herrschaft, varietals
  • Eastern Europe: Slovenia, Croatia, Hungary (Tokaji classified vineyards & vintages)
  • England & Wales: Vineyard sites and principal wines

New World

15 Australia

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Geographical features & influence on regions
  • Principal soils of major regions
  • Advanced vinification (acidification, de-alcoholisation)
  • Sub-regions ALL districts. Tasmania sparkling
  • Principal producers and wines — all districts
16 New Zealand

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Geographical features & influence
  • GI System — all districts and sub-districts
  • Principal grape plantings all regions
  • Principal producers, estates, locations
17 United States & Canada

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • AVAs of California (by district). Smaller regions (Mendocino, Lake, Livermore, etc.)
  • Ontario VQA/DVA. Niagara Peninsula. British Columbia
  • Ice Wine production & vintages
  • New York State: Finger Lakes, Long Island
  • Major producers & varietals each district
18 South America

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Geographical features (Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay, Mexico)
  • Soils. Wine production at altitude
  • DO areas all South American countries
  • Notable vineyards. Prestige labels. Ageing laws
  • Native grapes including Criollas
  • Principal producers. Latest developments
19 South Africa

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Geographical features (Mountains, Oceans, Rivers)
  • Location of wards in ALL WO districts
  • Principal producers, sites & wines — all regions
  • Vintages 10 years. Latest developments
20 Other (China · Middle East · Japan)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • China: districts, grapes, producers
  • Israel, Turkey, Cyprus, Lebanon: production & wines
  • Commandaria: production & quality levels
  • Japan (Koshu): geographic factors, production techniques

Fortified · Spirits · Beverages

21 Fortified Wines (Sherry · Port · Madeira)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Detailed production: fortification, sweetening, colouring
  • Sherry styles, ageing designations, Pagos
  • Port: vinification, vineyard rating, single quintas, vintages 1970–present
  • Madeira: producers, styles (3/5/10/15yr, Fresqueira, Colheita)
  • Vermouths. VdL (Floc, Ratafia, Pineau). VDN ACs
  • Moscatel de Setúbal. Australian Muscats. Commandaria. Montilla. Málaga
  • New styles: Añada, En Rama. Quinta classification (A-F)
22 Spirits

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Principal producers/brands all types
  • Rum production & principal types/producers
  • Eastern European spirits. Fruit eau de vie — names by country
  • Technical production details. Regulations
  • Location of Scottish distilleries
  • Styles & ageing requirements all spirit types
23 Liqueurs · Aperitifs · Bitters

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Liqueurs by type, colour & flavour. Brands/producers
  • Service requirements. Use in cocktails & mixed drinks
24 Beers & Ciders

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Malt types & production. Best known hop varieties
  • Brewing: Dry Hopping, Kräusening
  • World beer types: Lambic, Rauchbier, Seasonal, Gueuze
25 Saké

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Production areas. Base materials
  • Brewing terms: Moto, Moromi, Shikomi, Shubo, Nihonshu-do, Kimoto, Yamahai, Toji
  • Principal types of Saké

Technical & Professional Skills

26 Viticulture & Vinification

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Soil composition ALL regions & effect on grapes
  • Climatic effects: altitude, fogs, sun exposure
  • Vine diseases & prevention (Nematodes, Mealybugs, Esca, Pierce's, Eutypa, etc.)
  • Vine spacing, pruning, trellising
  • Technical winemaking (Reverse osmosis, de-alcoholisation, fining, filtration)
  • Natural, Organic, Biodynamic techniques & terms
  • Barrel sizes & ageing requirements. Effect of screwcaps
27 Food & Wine Pairing

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Match wines with vintages & producers, with reasoning
  • Matching vs contrasting. Best/off vintages
  • Pairings with beers, fortified, aperitifs
  • Alternatives & similar wines. Staff training. Customer skills
28 Business Aspects

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Spreadsheet calculations for inventory
  • Purchasing systems (wholesale & importation)
  • Sales & promotion. Staff training programmes
  • Fine wine list & function wine list structure
  • Tested in both Theory and Practical
29 Practical Service (45 min exam)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Customer & selling skills. Complaints & enquiries
  • Aperitif & cocktail preparation (comprehensive cocktail knowledge)
  • Wine recommendations all menus & cuisines
  • Champagne/sparkling service. Professional decanting
  • Digestif service. Equipment maintenance
  • Calculate profit margins. Inventory. Beverage requirements
  • Lead training programmes. Taste & identify beverages
30 Deductive Tasting (6 wines · 25 min · oral)

🎯 Advanced — Study Focus

  • Wide range of grape varietal characteristics
  • Oral description: technical & marketing format
  • Court format: Sight, Nose, Palate
  • Primary, Secondary, Tertiary elements
  • Structure: Alcohol, Acidity, Tannins (quantitative)
  • Age characteristics. Conclusion: Style, Country/Region, Quality, Vintage
  • Wine faults: Brett, Oxidation, Reduction, TCA. Correction methods
